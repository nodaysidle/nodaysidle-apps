# antislop Delivery Gate: Lights out redesign

Date: 2026-10-19. Mode: during (global preference). Build: `SHOW_ALL=1 npm run build`, served with `npm run dev` on port 4173. Checked with a Playwright script at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide, across `/`, all 8 app pages and a missing path.

Design read: an app showcase and download site for developers and Linux and macOS power users coming from X, in the "Lights out" language (black, white, one red). Dials: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Direction came from Alan and Kaly in the brainstorm (mockups `homepage-v6.html` and `app-page-v2.html`); the spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-lights-out-redesign-design.md`.

## Block 3: Liveliness

- **Dials are declared:** ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The output matches them. The headline is huge; the homepage and the app pages share one system, with the next-app link and the icon row as the deliberate breaks; the only motion is the lights and hover.
- **Focal point per screen:** the headline on `/`, the app name and icon on app pages.
- **Whitespace is structural:** it separates the headline, the icon row and the footer.
- **One accent:** red `#e10600`, used only for the lights and the button hover.
- **Identity motif:** the five start lights (big on `/`, small and dark in the app page bar), together with the greyscale icons that come alive.
- **Design read declared:** in the spec and above.

## Block 4: Craftsmanship and Quality Locks

All checks are clean:

- **No template sections:** there's no hero with two CTAs, no features grid, no logo bar, no pricing, no four-column footer.
- **No pill shapes and no generic CTAs:** the button says "Download vX.Y.Z on GitHub" or "Open the app".
- **No buzzwords:** a grep of `dist` for seamless, revolutionary, AI powered, get started, learn more and similar found nothing.
- **Palette:** black, white and one red, plus greys.
- **Not a clone of a known product,** and every major choice has a written reason in the spec.

## Not covered by this gate

- **Download apps have no demo video.** The old media was title cards (icon plus name plus a stale version), not app footage. Real clips are REEL's job, and the page shows them once `media.mp4` is added.
- **The Vercel preview deployment is untested.** Everything above was run against the local build; the preview deployment is checked in Task 7.
